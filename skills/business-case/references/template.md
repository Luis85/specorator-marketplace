## Executive summary

Decision requested, chosen option, whole-life cost, headline benefit, NPV, payback, and the
single biggest risk to the case. Half a page.

## 1. Strategic case — the case for change

- Business need and evidence (volumes, costs, failure rates, obligations).
- Strategic fit: which organisational objective, regulation, or portfolio goal, and who owns it.
- What business as usual looks like over the appraisal period.
- Scope of the investment, at outcome level.

## 8. Commercial case

Procurement route, contract type and why, supplier market and competition, key contract
terms and incentives, payment structure, exit and IP position, risk transfer.

## 9. Financial case

Funding source and approval status, affordability against the actual budget line, capital
vs operating treatment, cash-flow timing by period, VAT/tax treatment if material,
what happens if funding lands late.

## 10. Management case

Governance and gates, delivery approach, project manager and board, benefits owner after
handover, transition and business-readiness arrangements, post-implementation review date,
key risks to deliverability with responses.

## 11. Conditions under which this case no longer holds

Explicit triggers: cost above X, benefit realisation delayed past Y, dependency Z cancelled,
regulation withdrawn. These become escalation triggers in delivery.
