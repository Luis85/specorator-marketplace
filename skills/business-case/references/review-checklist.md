# Review checklist — business case

✅ pass / ⚠️ weak / ❌ blocking. Recompute the headline numbers before commenting on them.

## Case for change

- [ ] Business need is evidenced with figures, not asserted.
- [ ] Strategic fit names a specific objective, obligation, or portfolio goal with an owner.
- [ ] Business-as-usual consequences are quantified over the appraisal period.
- [ ] The scope of the investment matches the scope in the brief/charter.

## Baseline

- [ ] A current-state table exists with measure, value, source, and date.
- [ ] Every quantified benefit maps to a baseline measure.
- [ ] Baseline figures are recent enough to still be true.

## Options

- [ ] Do-nothing and do-minimum are both present.
- [ ] Do-nothing is fairly costed (it has consequences, not zero cost).
- [ ] Options are compared on the same basis and period.
- [ ] The rationale for the preferred option references the comparison, and names what is given up.
- [ ] At least one option was seriously considered that is not "the thing we already decided".

## Benefits

- [ ] Each benefit has measure, baseline, target, realisation date, measurement method.
- [ ] Each benefit has a named owner **in the receiving business**, not the project team.
- [ ] Cash-releasing and non-cash-releasing benefits are separated and not summed.
- [ ] Cash-releasing benefits are agreed by the budget holder whose line will fall.
- [ ] Ramp-up is modelled; no full benefit from day one without justification.
- [ ] No benefit double-counted with another project or another line in this case.
- [ ] Benefits requiring business change have that change funded and owned somewhere.
- [ ] Dis-benefits are listed and distinguished from risks.

## Costs

- [ ] Whole-life: delivery, transition, operating, and decommissioning.
- [ ] Internal effort costed at a stated rate with the FTE assumption visible.
- [ ] Recurring costs cover the full appraisal period.
- [ ] Contingency has a stated basis (risk EMV or evidenced uplift), and sits outside the base estimate.
- [ ] Business-change cost included, not just technical delivery.

## Financials

- [ ] Discount rate, appraisal period, and price base are stated.
- [ ] NPV, BCR, payback, and ROI are internally consistent with the cost and benefit tables.
- [ ] Arithmetic checks out on a spot recomputation.
- [ ] Sensitivity covers benefits low, costs high, and delivery late.
- [ ] A switching value / break-even point is identified.
- [ ] Optimism bias is addressed explicitly (reference class, comparable outturns, or a stated uplift).

## Commercial and financial viability

- [ ] Procurement route and contract type named, with a reason.
- [ ] Payment structure and risk transfer described.
- [ ] Funding source named and its approval status stated.
- [ ] Affordability tested against the actual budget line and cash-flow timing.

## Management viability

- [ ] Governance, gates, and decision rights named.
- [ ] Benefits owner after handover named.
- [ ] Post-implementation review scheduled with a date.
- [ ] Delivery risks to the case have responses, not just descriptions.

## Maintenance

- [ ] The conditions that would invalidate the case are written down.
- [ ] The case shows when it was last updated and against which stage.
- [ ] Assumptions carry impact-if-wrong, owner, and resolve-by date.

## Common findings, phrased usefully

| Finding | Say it like this |
|---|---|
| Unbaselined benefit | "B2 targets 20% fewer support calls with no current call volume. Add the baseline and its source, or reclassify B2 as unquantifiable." |
| Summed benefit types | "§4 totals €900k by adding cash-releasing savings to 1,100 freed hours. Split the total; capacity is not cash unless a budget line falls." |
| Contingency with no basis | "10% contingency appears with no derivation. Either sum the risk EMVs from the register or cite comparable-project overruns." |
| Straw-man do-nothing | "Option 0 shows zero cost, but the current licence renews at €180k in Year 2. Cost do-nothing properly — it may change the ranking." |
| Unowned realisation | "No benefit owner is named after handover. Nominate the operations manager for B1–B3 and add the measurement date to their reporting." |
| Optimism untested | "Sensitivity moves costs ±5%. Comparable projects here overran 40%. Test at +40% and state whether the case survives." |
