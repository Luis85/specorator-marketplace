# Review checklist — statement of work

Read it twice: once as the buyer, once as the supplier. ✅ pass / ⚠️ weak / ❌ blocking.

## Structure and hierarchy

- [ ] Parties are legal entities; the governing agreement is cited with a date.
- [ ] Precedence order between agreement, SOW, annexes, and proposal is stated.
- [ ] Version and effective date present; period of performance explicit.

## Deliverables

- [ ] Every deliverable is a **noun**, not an activity.
- [ ] Each has a format, a recipient, and a due date or an objective trigger.
- [ ] Each maps to at least one testable acceptance criterion.
- [ ] Nothing in the scope narrative implies a deliverable missing from the table.
- [ ] Interim and final deliverables are distinguished.

## Acceptance

- [ ] Review window stated in business days.
- [ ] Named signatory with authority to accept and to reject.
- [ ] Rejection produces written reasons within a stated period.
- [ ] Rework is at supplier cost, with a cycle limit and a remedy beyond it.
- [ ] Deemed-acceptance clause is present or deliberately absent, and its window is reasonable.
- [ ] Criteria are objective — verifiable by test, inspection, demonstration, or document review.

## Scope boundary

- [ ] Numbered exclusions list exists.
- [ ] Assumptions are numbered and each states the consequence if untrue.
- [ ] No assumption silently transfers scope back to the buyer without a change route.

## Buyer obligations

- [ ] Listed with owner, needed-by date, and effect if late.
- [ ] Covers access, environments, data, decisions, SME time, approvals.
- [ ] Relief mechanism for late buyer inputs is symmetrical and specific (day-for-day, standby rate).

## Schedule

- [ ] Milestones have completion criteria, not just dates.
- [ ] Dependencies on buyer inputs are visible in the milestone table.
- [ ] Hard external dates are identified with their driver.
- [ ] The schedule is achievable given the stated start date and lead times.

## Commercials

- [ ] Pricing model named and appropriate to scope certainty (see contract-and-pricing.md).
- [ ] Total or not-to-exceed figure stated.
- [ ] Every payment has an objectively evidenced trigger.
- [ ] Payment terms name the event that starts the clock.
- [ ] Rate card includes hours-per-day; expenses policy is capped or excluded.
- [ ] Currency, tax treatment, and indexation for multi-year work addressed.
- [ ] For capped T&M: what happens on reaching the cap is written down.

## Change control

- [ ] A written variation route exists, with an impact-assessment window.
- [ ] Authorisation thresholds and approvers named.
- [ ] Unauthorised work is explicitly non-chargeable.
- [ ] Pricing basis for variations (rate card or fixed uplift) is stated.

## People and governance

- [ ] Named contacts on both sides for delivery, acceptance, and escalation.
- [ ] Key personnel named; substitution and subcontracting rules stated.
- [ ] Reporting content, frequency, and recipient defined.
- [ ] Escalation ladder has response times.

## Compliance, data, IP

- [ ] Applicable standards and regulations named.
- [ ] Security obligations and incident-notification window stated.
- [ ] Personal-data roles, purposes, locations, sub-processors, and retention covered.
- [ ] Ownership of outputs and licences to pre-existing/third-party/open-source components clear.
- [ ] Source materials and documentation are deliverables where the buyer will need them.
- [ ] AI-tool use and human-review obligations addressed where relevant.

## Exit

- [ ] Termination for convenience and for cause, with notice periods.
- [ ] Payment position on termination.
- [ ] Hand-over deliverables listed (documentation, credentials, data export, knowledge transfer).
- [ ] Transition support duration and rate.

## Overall

- [ ] No "as mutually agreed" on anything that costs money or time.
- [ ] No contradiction with the master agreement — or if there is, precedence resolves it.
- [ ] A stranger could determine, from this document alone, whether the supplier performed.
- [ ] Reviewer has flagged that material commercial and liability terms need legal review.

## Common findings, phrased usefully

| Finding | Say it like this |
|---|---|
| Verb deliverable | "D2 is 'provide architecture support' — unmissable and unacceptable. Replace with 'Target architecture document, reviewed at …, meeting AC4–AC6'." |
| Open acceptance | "§4 says the buyer 'will review promptly'. Set 10 business days, a named signatory, and the consequence of no response." |
| Unbounded rework | "Rework is unlimited at supplier cost with no exit. Add a two-cycle limit, then price reduction or deliverable termination." |
| Missing buyer duties | "No buyer obligations are listed, yet D1 needs production-like test data. Add it with an owner, a date, and day-for-day relief." |
| Payment on 'completion' | "P2 triggers on 'Phase 2 completion' — undefined. Trigger on written acceptance of D5 and D6." |
| Proposal wins | "The precedence clause places the supplier proposal above this SOW, importing its assumptions. Invert the order." |
