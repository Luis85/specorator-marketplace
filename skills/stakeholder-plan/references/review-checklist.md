# Review checklist — stakeholder and communications plan

✅ pass / ⚠️ weak / ❌ blocking.

## Coverage

- [ ] Register goes beyond the governance chain to those affected operationally.
- [ ] Operations, support, and service desk are present.
- [ ] Data owners, security, privacy, procurement, and finance considered.
- [ ] Users' line managers present (they decide whether adoption actually happens).
- [ ] Adjacent projects and shared-dependency owners present.
- [ ] Suppliers, partners, regulators, and customers considered where relevant.
- [ ] Anyone with a veto or approval authority is named.
- [ ] Groups consciously excluded are noted as excluded, not simply absent.

## Register quality

- [ ] Each entry states what they care about, what they need, and what we need from them.
- [ ] Current position recorded (unaware / resistant / neutral / supportive / leading).
- [ ] Target position recorded, and it differs from current where it should.
- [ ] A named relationship owner per stakeholder — no unowned high-power entries.
- [ ] Individuals named where the relationship is with a person, not just a group label.
- [ ] Nothing recorded that could not be defended to the person concerned.

## Analysis

- [ ] Power/interest placement is argued, not decorative.
- [ ] Attitude captured as well as power and interest.
- [ ] Where politics are contested, legitimacy and urgency are considered too.
- [ ] Influence network noted — who moves whose opinion.
- [ ] Analysis is dated and scheduled for re-review.

## From analysis to action

- [ ] Every current→target gap has at least one engagement action.
- [ ] Actions have owners and dates.
- [ ] Resistant stakeholders have their concern stated in their own words, and the legitimate
      part of it acknowledged.
- [ ] The sponsor is asked to use their influence on something specific, not just to be copied in.

## Decision rights

- [ ] RACI covers contested decisions and deliverables, not tasks.
- [ ] Exactly one Accountable per row.
- [ ] Consulted parties have a response deadline.
- [ ] The matrix has been validated with the named people.
- [ ] Fewer than about eight rows; each one is a decision that will actually be argued over.

## Communications plan

- [ ] Audiences are specific; no "all staff" row.
- [ ] Each row states purpose (inform / consult / involve / collaborate).
- [ ] Sender is the person with credibility for that audience, not a mailbox.
- [ ] Channel uses existing forums where they exist.
- [ ] Frequency rises as impact approaches.
- [ ] Every audience has a named feedback route.
- [ ] Purpose promised matches purpose delivered (no "consult" that is really "inform").
- [ ] Time-critical dates identified with who prepares and who approves.
- [ ] Accessibility and language needs addressed for affected groups.
- [ ] Bad-news handling is covered — who says what if a date slips.

## Common findings, phrased usefully

| Finding | Say it like this |
|---|---|
| Missing inheritor | "The support team that will take calls from day one is not in the register. Add them as involve-level from T−12, owned by the delivery lead." |
| Unowned power | "The finance director can block the funding release and has no relationship owner or scheduled contact. Assign to the sponsor with a monthly 1:1." |
| Broadcast only | "All eight communications rows are one-way. Two audiences are being asked to change how they work — move them to consult with a named feedback contact." |
| Fake audience | "'All staff' cannot be written for. Split into head-office users, branch users, and field staff; the change differs for each." |
| Multiple accountable | "Three rows of the RACI have two Accountables. Pick one per decision or the decision will not get made." |
| Unclosed gap | "Six stakeholders are Resistant with target Supportive and no engagement action. Add an action, owner, and date for each, or lower the target and accept the consequence." |
| Promise mismatch | "The plan promises to 'consult' the works council but the design is already fixed. Either genuinely consult or state that this is an inform." |
